Privacy Policy

Chronos Lifelog - Private Edge Geotemporal Timeline Engine

Effective Date: November 04, 2026

1. Zero-Cloud Privacy Guarantee

Chronos Lifelog is built from the ground up on the principle of Zero-Cloud Execution. The application binary completely omits the android.permission.INTERNET declaration. Under the Android operating system security model, the app is physically incapable of opening network sockets, pinging remote servers, transmitting telemetry, or sending data to third parties.

2. Location & Motion Data

Chronos Lifelog requests ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION and ACTIVITY_RECOGNITION permissions strictly to compute on-device spatio-temporal trajectories and identify dwell clusters locally on your phone. All coordinates, elevation tracks, and activity classifications are stored in an on-device SQLite database encrypted with AES-256 via the hardware Android StrongBox enclave. No location data ever leaves your device.

3. Audio Sensor Policy (Zero Raw Audio Recording)

Chronos Lifelog utilizes the microphone solely to calculate momentary sound pressure levels (SPL decibels) and spectral variance to categorize ambient environments (such as library, street, or train). Raw audio is never recorded, never transcribed, never saved to disk, and never transmitted. All audio buffers are sampled volatilely in RAM and discarded within milliseconds.

4. Data Sovereignty & Deletion

You have absolute sovereignty over your lifelog data. You can export your full geotemporal history to standard GeoJSON at any time, or execute a Cryptographic Zero-Fill Purge from the Airgap Shield screen to permanently overwrite and destroy all local logs.
